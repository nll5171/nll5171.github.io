EarthCam Laravel Demo

Check out the project source here on GitHub! Or view it in action here.

Before starting this project, I had applied for the Jr. Full Stack Developer role with EarthCam, a company specializing in webcam technology services, such as the use of computer vision technology to analyze construction sites and detect potential accidents before they happen. Though I was told I performed well during the interview stage, I was passed up in favor of other candidates.

While I was never given an exact reason as to why, I believe it was my lack of knowledge of Laravel. EarthCam's tech stack revolved around the use of React.js, which I knew comfortably, MySQL, which I had worked with since I was a freshman in high school, and Laravel, which I had just about no experience with. Despite this, I claimed I was a quick learner and would be able to pick it up without much issue.

This project exists to try and prove that fact. When I had learned of the Jr. Backend Developer position they listed shortly afterwards, I took 3 days to learn as much as I could about Laravel, and another two days to build this application from the ground-up. I wanted to ensure I used as much of EarthCam's tech stack as possible, hence why the site was built using React.js, Laravel, and MySQL.

Since the front-end was slightly lower-priority, I used TailwindCSS in tandem with daisyUI to rapidly put together UI components. This was not my first time working with TailwindCSS, but it was my first time using daisyUI, which was great.

Ultimately, this project performs very basic CRUD operations on what I'll refer to as "reviews" essentially. There's a basic authentication system as well, middleware guards on the backend to prevent creation, updating, and deleting without an account, backend form validation, and a few other things. All the sorts of things you'd expect from a very basic application.

Since I also had quite a few Heroku credits remaining from my time as a university student with the Eco Dynos plan, I decided to host the project publicly. If you are a member of EarthCam reading this, I sincerely hope you take me into consideration for this role. I believe strongly in my ability to meet and exceed the needs of this role, and I hope this project helped to prove that I am a quick learner, and eager to improve as a developer.
