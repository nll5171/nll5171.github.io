Green Shields in Under Night In-Birth 2

A video detailing the changes made to the green shield mechanic of Under Night In-Birth during the transition between the first and second game. To elaborate a bit more on green shields, we first have to discuss shielding, an action that can be performed on defense.

By inputting the D button prior to blocking, you reduce the amount of time you are stuck blocking by 3 frames. You also gain GRD, this game's tug of war mechanic that provides numerous benefits to whoever wins it. If you are late however, you get a green shield instead, which increases your time spent blocking significantly and causes you to lose GRD instead.

Ultimately, this was a fairly quick project I put together within the span of about two days. I had already known where to look within the game's code to identify what changed between games, and given the video's length, it wasn't that much. Green shielding is also a fairly simple mechanic in the grand scheme of things, so there's only so much to cover with something like that.

It was a fun video to produce within the relatively short amount of free time I had to make it. Editing as always is the longest portion of the process, and that went even more so here, given that I didn't need to analyze assembly code like I had with the last video on this game series.
